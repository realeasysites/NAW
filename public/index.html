What comes out of the shop

Performance and fab work, not oil changes. If it needs to make more power, sit lower, run harder or get welded right, it belongs here.

Custom fabrication and TIG welding

Stainless exhaust, downpipes, intake and charge piping, brackets and one-off parts. Clean, even beads you won't want to hide under a heat wrap.

Engine swaps and turbo builds

LS swaps, twin-turbo setups, Cummins swaps and compound-turbo diesels. Mounts, plumbing, wiring and fuel handled together so the whole thing works on day one.

Chassis, suspension and cages

4-link rear setups, front suspension, roll cages and cage bars. The structural work that keeps big power pointed straight and keeps the driver safe.

Drag and track prep

Builds don't stop at the shop door. These cars go down the strip, and they're set up to go back for more.

Muscle cars and classics

Mustangs, Novas, C10s and Pontiacs. Engine rebuilds, upgrades and installs done with the care a classic deserves.

The red Nova

A classic Chevy Nova with a twin-turbo LS, Holley EFI and hand-built stainless turbo plumbing. Built in the shop, then taken where it was made to go: the track.
